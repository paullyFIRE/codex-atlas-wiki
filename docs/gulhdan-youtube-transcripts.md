## Game Concepts Extracted

### 1. Account & Progression Basics (Video 1)
- **Account linking**: Link to 89 Trillion (parent company), Facebook, or Google Play to prevent data loss
- **Nickname**: Free unlimited changes
- **Invite codes**: Enter another player's code for 100 free gems
- **Leveling**: Milestones at 11, 21, 31, 61 — reaches 61 quickly
- **Auto-display feature**: Use community-made templates. Three modes: Balanced Power, Power First, Solid Fortress
- **Training mode**: Test teams against yourself to iterate

### 2. Command Center / Castle (Videos 1, 4)
- Unlocks at level 61 at level 1
- **Priority #1** — upgrade to level 4 ASAP, then 5
- Expands grid: 3x3 → 4x4 → 5×4 → 7×6 (42 slots at level 5)
- Only spend wood on castle requirements (sawmill, mine, etc.) — wood is the scarcest resource
- Generates passive gems (caps at 100 storage at level 5 = 240 gems/day collecting every ~6h)
- Determines energy cap for deploying troops

### 3. Resource System (Videos 1, 4)
- **Wood**: Scarcest resource. Get from co-op (300-600 per match) vs story (30-50 per match)
- **Gold**: Used for common fusion and shop purchases
- **Gems**: Premium currency — detailed breakdown below
- **Silver/Plata**: Used in co-op for mine upgrades

### 5. Fusion System (Videos 1, 3, 5)
**Core mechanic**: Combine 3 cards of same tier → 1 card of next tier at level+0

**Gem costs per fusion**:
| Input → Output | Cost |
|---|---|
| 3 Common → 1 Uncommon | Gold only |
| 3 Uncommon → 1 Epic | 30 gems |
| 3 Epic → 1 Legendary | 75 gems |
| 3 Legendary → 1 Mystic | 450 gems |

**Card count per level**: 2^n (level 1 = 1 card, level 2 = 2, level 6 = 32 cards)

**Cost for level 6 Mystic via fusion**: 450 × 32 = **14,400 gems** (most gem-efficient)

**7 Fusion Mystics available**:
| Hero | Role | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| Griffin Knight (Caballero del Grifo) | Tank | 2 at level 6 — stun ability good |
| Geomancer (Geomante) | — | 1-2 at level 6, situational |
| Storm Maiden (Doncella de la Tormenta) | — | 1-2 at level 6, strong |
| Flame Duelist (Duelista de Llamas) | DPS | 2-3 at level 6 (highest priority) |
| Artillery Gunner (Artillero Furioso) | DPS | Worst of the 7, still usable |
| Apostle (Apóstol) | Healer | 2 at level 6 (sufficient) |
| King Knight (Rey Caballero) | Tank | 2-3 at level 6 (high priority) |

### 6. Roulette / Wheel System (Videos 3, 5)
**Cost**: 100 gems per spin

**Fixed rewards**:
| Spins | Reward |
|---|---|
| 10 | 1 Mystic hero |
| 25 | 500 gems refund |
| 50 | 1 Mystic hero |
| 100 | 2 Mystic heroes |
| 200 | 500 forge stones |

**Real probabilities** (not the displayed 4%):
- 0.5% chance of 2 Mystic cards
- 3.5% chance of 1 Mystic card
- **True rate: 3.5%** per spin

**Cost analysis** (100 spins = 10,000 gems, net 9,500 after 500 refund):

| Spent | Expected Mystics | Cost per Mystic |
|---|---|---|
| 1,000 gems (10 spins) | 1 (guaranteed) | 1,000 |
| 2,500 → 2,000 net (25 spins) | ~1-2 | ~1,000 |
| 5,000 → 4,500 net (50 spins) | ~4 | ~1,125 |
| 10,000 → 9,500 net (100 spins) | ~8 | ~1,187 |
| 19,500 net (200 spins) | ~13 | ~1,500 |

**Comparison: Fusion vs Roulette** (10,000 gems):
- Roulette: ~8 Mystic cards (various heroes, RNG)
- Fusion: 22 Mystic cards (guaranteed, pool of 7 heroes)

**Recommendations**:
- **Don't touch roulette** until you have 6+ fusion mystics at level 6
- Spin 10 only if very low on gems (get the guaranteed mystic)
- **NEVER stop at 25** — doubled cost per mystic if you can't reach 50
- 50 is the minimum worthwhile breakpoint
- 100 only for midgame+ players with solid base
- 200 is never worth it even for forge stones

### 7. Co-op Mode Strategy — Level 89 (Video 2)
**The Mine Strategy** (taught by guildmate Juan PB):

**Core mechanic**: Use the dice mini-game instead of spending silver on units. Save ALL silver for mine upgrades.

**Mine upgrade targets**:
| Mine | Level | Income |
|---|---|---|
| Mine 1 | 5 | 5 gold / 15s |
| Mine 2 | 8 | 8 gold / 12s |
| Mine 3 | 8 | 8 gold / 9s |
| Mine 4 | 9 | 9 gold / 6s |
| Mine 5 | 20 | 20 gold / 3s |
| **Total** | | **~50 gold / 6-7s** |

**Key units to prioritize**:
- **DPS**: Blue mushrooms (hongos azules) — highest damage, position to focus boss
- **Stall**: Ursas (stun) + White-hair witches (tornado/slow) — position behind to delay waves
- **Frontline**: Fire sword warriors — full line at top for wave clear

**Boss strategy**: Level 89 boss is **never killed** — just survive. Stack all damage on mushrooms and keep stalling units between boss and DPS.

**Team coordination**:
- Angel choice: Both pick **middle**
- Demon choice: One picks **left**, other picks **right** (get different units)

### 8. Hero Rarity & Upgrade Path (Videos 1, 3, 5)
**Rarity tiers**: Common → Uncommon → Epic → Legendary → Mystic

**Max level**: Level 9 per unit

**Stats per card contain**:
- Attack, Defense, HP, Speed (per-level growth)
- Skill scaling with level
- RNG-based skill procs (e.g., Artillery Gunner: 12% chance per hit for AoE 180% damage + 30% ATK/ASPD boost, stacks 3x)

**Power system**: Troop power = combined rating. Auto-display generates templates based on unit levels and synergy.

### 9. PvP & Arena (Video 1)
- **Two rankings**: Group (50 players) + Global (all players)
- Gulhdan's stats: 4,710 cups, group rank #1, global rank ~1,123
- Defense team: ~80,000 power
- Attack team: ~78,000 power
- **Heavy RNG** — same matchup can go either way
- Best team-building advice: copy opponents who beat you, test in training mode

### 10. Battle Pass (Videos 1, 4)
- **Seasons**: 14 days each (e.g., Season 25)
- **Mission types**: Daily (reset every day), Weekly (1 per week = 2 per season), Seasonal (8 per season)
- Mission reroll: 3 free resets per day
- Free track vs Premium track rewards
- Gulhdan spends ~$60/month: $30 daily mystic pack + $5 basic pass + $15 advanced pass + $10 battle pass × 2

### 12. Key Non-Mystic Cards to Farm (Video 5)
Via common fusion:
- **Bomb**: AoE stun + damage — meta at all levels, seen everywhere in PvP
- **Goblin/Duende**: Solid early tank
- **Healer**: 2-3 copies at level 6 sufficient

Via legendary fusion:
- **Royal Archer**: Best card in the entire game (including mystics). 1-in-8 chance when fusing 3 level 6 legendaries (960 gems per attempt, ~7,680 expected for one)

### 13. Monetization & Spending Advice (Video 4)
- **$30/month daily mystic pack**: Most efficient way to buy mystics ($1/mystic, 30/month)
- **Basic gem pass ($5)**: 100 gems/day + 600 upfront
- **Advanced gem pass ($15)**: 320 gems/day + 2,000 upfront
- **Battle pass ($10/14 days)**: Worth it for gems
- **VIP privilege**: Not worth, skip
- **Growth fund**: Buy only if level 35-40+
- **3-mystic bundle ($10)**: Appears after first mystic summon — good value
- **Dragon house packs**: Best for dragon materials
