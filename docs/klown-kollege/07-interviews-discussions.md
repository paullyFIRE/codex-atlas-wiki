## Klown Interviews a Whale (Mshly)

### Background
- Player: Mshly (pronounced "Mllly" — first initial + last name)
- Clans: Sky Titans → Titan Warfare → Brotherhood → Hunters
- Playing ~10 months at time of recording
- One of the oldest active players

### Whale Perspective

**On Spending:**
- Whales spend heavily to stay competitive
- New mythics (Light Seeker, Necromancer) force even more spending to keep up
- "All of a sudden I'm getting beat by people that never came within 40 troops of me before — just because they have two more Light Seekers"

**On the State of the Meta:**
- Significant power spike with new mythics
- Accounts with these troops are disproportionately strong
- Creates pressure to keep spending
- "Great for the devs" (sarcastic)

**On the Game Longevity:**
- Playing 10 months, seen the game evolve
- Player retention issues noted — several big players quitting
- Whale spending supports the game economy

## Round Table Discussion (Salem, DudeMan, Mshly)

### Participants
- **Clown** (Heretic Sanctuary, host)
- **Mshly** (Sky Titans)
- **DudeMan** (4F)
- **Salem** (Heretic Sanctuary)

### Topic: Are the New Mythics (Light Seeker, Necromancer) a Problem?

| Panelist | Verdict | Reasoning |
|----------|---------|-----------|
| Mshly | Yes (but good for devs) | Being beaten by weaker players with more Light Seekers |
| DudeMan | Problem | "If you want a game that rewards logging in every day, probably a problem. If you want to be immediately the strongest with no thought, not a problem." |
| Salem | Yes, it's a problem | "There's no balance anymore. Sloppy formations wreak havoc. Takes the countless hours others put in over months and makes it meaningless." |

### Consensus
- Clear power spike with the new mythics
- Light Seeker and Necromancer at level 4 provide value that used to require level 6
- This makes the game more pay-to-win
- But the devs need to monetize somehow
- The troops are "strong as hell"
- Mindless formations with these troops beat well-constructed older formations

### Discussion Highlights
- The troops are "strong as hell" and it's "a problem" for those who don't keep up
- Great for devs (revenue), bad for game balance
- Sloppy/careless formations are winning against carefully crafted ones
- Value the countless hours put in by older players has been diminished

## Collab with Vinnie OG (Old Guy) — Clan War Discussion

### Participants
- **Clown** (Heretic Sanctuary)
- **Vinnie OG** (4F / Four Funds)
- Bonded over being '85 babies, 311 music, and making helping videos

### Clan War: The Good

**What's great about clan war:**
- The flagship game mode — "it's why we spend gems, it's why we spend money"
- The rush of 20-30 people showing up to hit an objective
- "There's a dopamine hit right there"
- "An ant farm on the map" — everyone running around
- Strategic coordination between teams
- Teamwork and camaraderie

### Clan War: The Bad

**Issues discussed:**
- Points system heavily skewed toward dragon hits
- Scheduling dragon times can be frustrating
- Not all teams cooperate with dragon diplomacy
- Strong teams can dominate without coordination
- Newer teams feel overwhelmed

### Clan War: The Ugly

**Deep issues:**
- Player retention — big teams dissolving or combining
- Long-standing players stepping down from leadership, taking breaks, or quitting
- Drama within top clans causing splits (Mortal Crowns → Heretic Sanctuary)
- "Losing some good players"

### Overall Sentiment
Despite the issues, both agree clan war is the best part of the game. It needs some refinement but the core experience is excellent.
