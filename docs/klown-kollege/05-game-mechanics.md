## Light vs Dark Faction Analysis

### Necromancer (Dark)

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Strengths | PvE (hunting, Infinite War, dragon). Skeletons deal massive AoE damage. |
| Weaknesses | High morale loss on dragon (~176 vs ~121 normal). Needs Goddess of War to survive. |
| Morale cost | ~45% more morale per dragon hit |
| Dragon damage | Up to 3.6M per hit |
| PvP | Viable with Goddess support. Strong player in Heretic Sanctuary uses him in PvP successfully. |

### Light Seeker (Light)

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Strengths | PvP dominance. Holy Wash: heal to 100% + AoE damage. Light Volley: multi-shot + 50% attack buff. |
| Weaknesses | Less AoE than Necromancer for PvE |
| PvE | Decent single target damage |
| PvP | S+ tier. Multiple copies shred through formations. |

### Conclusion
Both are good. Light Seeker is stronger in PvP; Necromancer is stronger in PvE. If you can only pick one, consider your priority. If you can get both, do it.

## Building Guide

### Priority Order

1. **Castle / Command Center** — ABSOLUTE #1 PRIORITY
   - Every level gives +50 troop energy
   - More energy = more dragons worth of power
   - "Ten dragons worth of power" per upgrade
   - Spend ALL wood on this until a few levels deep

2. **Spell Cauldron** — Unlocks at certain castle level
   - Build it as soon as available

3. **Gold Mine** — Steady gold income
   - Collect every 15 seconds in co-op
   - Level up for more gold per tick

4. **Sawmill** — Wood production
   - Feeds castle upgrades

5. **Follower Camp** — Low priority
   - "At the moment isn't much use"
   - Can stay level 1

### Daily Building Routine
1. Log in
2. Bottom left → Building icon
3. Collect all resources
4. Start next castle upgrade
5. Queue other building upgrades

## Co-op Strategy

### Basics
- Team up with a partner (clanmate recommended)
- Gold mine: click and level for resources every 15 seconds
- Devil pops up: pick a DIFFERENT card than your partner
- Spawning: click spawned units to collect resources

### Gold Mine Level
- Keep at level 5 (conflicting info, but 5 is commonly recommended)
- Higher levels give more gold per tick

### Card Selection
- Pick different cards from your partner
- Coordinate picks for maximum resource gain
- "Test Complete" means you matched correctly

### Partner Choice
- Random matchmaking works (usually lower level players)
- Clanmates preferred for coordination

## Dragon Clash Event

### How It Works
- Limited-time event (starts mid-event, runs periodically)
- Pick a side: Red Dragon or Blue Dragon
- Both are good — Clown leans Blue
- Separate quest log and reward tree (like battle pass)
- Separate dragon battle pass available

### Why Join ASAP
- Don't wait like a "idiot" (Clown's mistake)
- If you don't pick a side, you miss quests
- Daily quests + reset available (same as normal)
- Reward tree mimics the normal battle pass

### Tips
- Pick immediately upon event start
- Do dragon-specific daily quests
- Claim the dragon battle pass rewards
- Both dragons have good abilities but lean Blue if unsure

## Exchange Shop (Caravan Camp)

### How It Works
- Limited-time event (returns periodically)
- Complete tasks → earn Caravan Coins
- 12 tasks total → 6,000 coins
- 1 week to complete all tasks
- Tasks are easy: just play normally

### Best Purchases (6,000 coins budget)

| Item | Cost | Priority |
|------|------|----------|
| Royal Archers (all 10) | 4,000 | Highest |
| Gem Scrolls (3) | 1,500 | High |
| 1 Scroll | 480 | Good |
| Arena Skin | ~500 | Cosmetic |

Total: 5,980 coins (20 left over)

### Other Options
- Frost Queen, Barbarian, Melody Weaver available in some rotations
- Arena scene skins (cosmetic — changes arena background)
- Exclusive avatar frames
- Frost Queen and Night King skins (season-themed)

### Recommendation
Royal Archers first — they're hard to get, basically only from wheel or paid packs. Scrolls second.

## In-Game Name Colors

### How To Change
1. Go to profile
2. Click to change name
3. Add color code at the start: `<#RGB>` where RGB is a 3-character hex code
4. Example: `<#F00>Clown` = red name
5. Your name must be short enough to fit with the code

### Finding Colors
- Website: borderleft.com/toolbox/hex
- Shows 3-digit hex codes for all colors

### Where Colors Show
- In-game name
- Clan war map (team color name)
- Chat messages
- Clan member list

### Team Names Also Work
Teams can use color codes in their team name as well.

## Fusion System

### How It Works
- Combine 3 troops of the same rarity and level → 1 random troop of next rarity
- Same level in = same level out
- Common → Uncommon → Rare → Epic → Legendary → Mythic

### Costs
| Fusion | Cost |
|--------|------|
| Common → Uncommon | Gold only |
| Uncommon → Rare | Gold only |
| Rare → Epic | Gems |
| Epic → Legendary | Gems |
| Legendary → Mythic | Gems |

### The Case for Fusion
- Good way to convert unwanted troops
- Only way to get Bombs consistently (uncommon/rare fusion)
- Can meet castle level requirements for owning high-level troops
- Cheaper to fuse to level 8 epics now with forge stone discounts

### The Case Against Fusion (Updated)
- Wheel and Card Master are better gem investments
- New mythics at level 4 are power spikes worth saving for
- Natural play + battle pass gives enough epics/legendaries
- Only use gold for uncommon/rare fusion

### Level 8 Epic Fusion Strategy (With Discounts)
1. Get rare to level 8 (288 forge stones)
2. Get another rare to level 8 (288 forge stones)
3. Fuse both → random level 8 epic
4. Risk: random, but all epics are usable
5. Poison Master, Flame Mage, Oracles are great pulls

### Important Note
Fusing away level 6 troops to get random level 6 of next rarity:
- If you fuse 3 level 6 uncommons → level 6 rare (Bomb chance!)
- Uncommon fusion costs only gold
- Common fusion costs only gold
