# Troop Tier List

Synthesized from the Full Troop Tier List, Infinite War Tiers, Hunting Troop Tiers, and weekly check-ins. Rankings reflect consensus from Clown, Salem, Vinnie OG, and DudeMan.

---

## S+ Tier (Best in Game)

These troops are universally strong across multiple game modes. Prioritize these for gem spending and upgrading.

| Troop | Why S+ |
|-------|--------|
| **Radiant Warrior** | S-tier utility. Level 6 ability gives all allies +20% resistance to all elements. Stack 2-4 in every formation. Shield + massive HP. |
| **Frost Queen** | Top 3 troop. Excellent in PvP and PvE. Keep two at same level, do NOT merge into one. |
| **Mistral Archer** | Consistent top performer. S+ tier in Full Tier List. |
| **Bone Marksman** | Pseudo level-6 at level 4 (skill unlocks at 4, not 6). Stats double 1→2→3→4. Scaling falls off at 4 (25-30% boost after). Excellent in PvP, hunting, clan war towers. |
| **Goddess of War** | Shields nearby troops. Essential for keeping Necromancer skeletons alive on dragon. Protects Oracles and backline. |
| **Light Seeker (Pretty Boy)** | Stronger raw damage than Necromancer in PvP. Holy Wash: heals self to 100% when below 50% HP, deals AoE damage. Level 4: Light Volley adds multi-shot + 50% attack buff. Multiple Light Seekers stack and shred. |
| **Necromancer** | Excels in PvE (hunting, Infinite War, dragon). Skeletons deal massive damage. PvP viable with Goddess of War support. ~176 morale loss on dragon vs ~121 normal. 3.6M dragon hits recorded. |

## S Tier (Excellent)

| Troop | Why S |
|-------|-------|
| **Oracles** | Pound-for-pound best epic in the game. 4% attack buff per Oracle to nearby allies, stacking up to 20 layers. With 9 Oracles: 36% boost. With 18: 72% boost. Essential buffer. |
| **Ursa** | Top legendary priority for forge stone upgrades. Level 7 Ursa is a must. |
| **Elven Archer** | Top legendary priority alongside Ursa. Level 7 recommended. Have 2-4. |
| **Darkmoon Queen** | S-tier alongside Frost Queen. |
| **Ripple Wizard** | Aqua Revival grants energy every 2.5s. Best paired with Storm Maiden, Frost Queen, Starlight Apostle, Jungle Ranger, Goddess of War. Hard to get (scrolls only). |
| **Flame Mage** | Excellent in clan war, hunting, clan hunt. Level 8 Flame Mage is very strong. |
| **Bomber (Bombs)** | Dominant in 2,500-5,000 trophy range. Fusion with gold is cheapest way to get them. |
| **Jungle Ranger** | Top buffer. Pairs with Ripple Wizard. |
| **Starlight Apostle** | Top buffer. Pairs with Ripple Wizard. |
| **Poison Master** | Carries early accounts — 8-10 level 6 Poison Masters can carry through mid-game Infinite War. |

## Infinite War Tier List (Specific)

| Tier | Troops |
|------|--------|
| **S** | Rock Throwers (8 troops = 8 targets), Goddess of War (shields), Poison Master (carries early) |
| **A** | Flame Mages, Royal Archers, Forest Scouts, Oracles |
| **B** | Warlocks, Marksmans, high-level Mists, Storm Maidens, Jungle Rangers |

Key Infinite War strategy:
- 2 full rows of tanks upfront
- 2-3 healers
- Rock Throwers spread enemy targeting
- Goddess of War shields the Rock Throwers
- Poison Masters carry until you get higher-impact troops

## Hunting Troop Tiers (Specific)

| Tier | Troops |
|------|--------|
| **S** | Single-target damage dealers: Flame Mages, Royal Archers |
| **A** | Forest Scouts, Bombs, Oracles |
| **B** | Buffers, tanks |

Hunting strategy:
- Stack all tanks on one side to shift aggro
- Best single-target damage troops perform best
- Flame Mages and Royal Archers excel
- Scouts at level 9 are effective (2.5 tile radius, placed in back for survival)
- Tank stacking on right side is the meta

## What to Invest In

1. **Radiant Warrior** — S+ tier, use 2-4 everywhere
2. **Frost Queen** — S+ tier, keep two
3. **Bone Marksman** — Get to level 4 minimum
4. **Goddess of War** — Essential support
5. **Oracles** — Best epic, stack as many as possible
6. **Ursa** (level 7) and **Elven Archer** (level 7) — best legendary upgrades
7. **Light Seeker** — PvP destroyer, multiple copies
8. **Necromancer** — PvE king, dragon damage
9. **Ripple Wizard** — Energy battery for your best troops
