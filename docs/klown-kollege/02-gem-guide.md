## What to Spend Gems On

### Primary: The Wheel

The wheel is the best gem sink. Each spin costs gems, with guaranteed mythic rewards at milestone chests.

**Wheel pull rates:**
- 4% chance per spin to get a mythic (~1 per 25 spins)
- 15% legendary
- Rest = epic, rare, coins

**Milestone chests (recommended stop points):**
- 10 pulls: first chest
- 30 pulls: second chest
- 50 pulls: significant reward
- 100 pulls: guaranteed mythic + extra rewards

Stopping at milestone chests (50 or 100) keeps your cost per mythic lower because you get guaranteed pieces.

### Secondary: Card Master

Cost analysis (from detailed number crunching using AI probability models):

- Card Master: ~250 gems per pull, 1/12 chance per draw for the featured mythic
- Average pulls to get the 1 mythic: 6.5
- For 32 mythics (to level a mythic to 6): ~208 pulls
- With chest rewards factored in, Card Master is not significantly more expensive than the wheel

**Verdict:** Wheel is slightly better if you stop at milestone chests. Card Master is fine if there are good troops in it. Use both.

### Secondary: Fusion (Partially)

Early guide recommended fusion. Updated guide reverses this.

**Old recommendation:** Fusion is a good way to spend gems
**New recommendation:** DO NOT spend gems on fusion. Only use gold.

Reasons for the change:
- New mythics at level 4 are power spikes worth saving for
- Wheel and Card Master give you good epics/legendaries anyway
- Battle pass + dailies + Infinite War give enough epics naturally
- Fusion is fine for castle level requirements (need level 5+ legendaries) but only ~1,000 gems

### What NOT to Spend Gems On

| Activity | Why Not |
|----------|---------|
| Extra Infinite War battles | "99% worthless unless you're at the very top" |
| Extra Hunting battles | Same as above |
| Recruit / Summon with gems | Better value elsewhere |
| Fusion with gems | Only use gold now |

## Wheel vs Card Draw Cost Comparison

Detailed breakdown from Clown's analysis:

**Card Master math:**
- 1 mythic per 12 cards, draw without replacement
- Average 6.5 picks to get the 1 mythic
- 32 mythics needed for level 6 → ~208 total picks
- 250 gems/pick → ~52,000 raw gems
- Chest rewards (every 10 pulls) reduce this significantly

**Wheel math:**
- 4% rate → 1 mythic per 25 spins on average
- 32 mythics → ~800 spins
- Milestone chests at 10, 30, 50, 100 give guaranteed mythics + forge stones
- Stopping at chest milestones lowers effective cost

**Bottom line:** Both are viable. Wheel at milestone chests is slightly more efficient. Card Master is not "absolute garbage" as initially believed.

## How to Get Bombs

Bombs are dominant in the 2,500-5,000 trophy range. Best way to get them:

1. **Fusion of uncommons** — cheapest method, costs only gold
   - Fuse 3 level 6 uncommons → random level 6 rare (can be a bomb)
   - Costs zero gems
   - Repeat with gold reserves

2. Sometimes appear on the wheel (not all wheels)

3. Fusion of commons/uncommons at any level gives a chance

## Fusion Guide

### Is Fusion Worth It?

**Yes, but only with gold, not gems.**

- Fuse 3 level 6 troops of one rarity → random level 6 troop of next rarity
- Uncommon → Rare fusion costs only gold
- Common → Uncommon fusion costs only gold
- Epic/Legendary fusion costs gems (generally not recommended)

**Bomb acquisition:** Fusion of uncommons is the best way to get bombs.

**Castle requirements:** Some castle levels require owning certain counts of level 5+ legendaries/epics. Fusion can help meet these requirements (~1,000 gem investment if needed).

**Updated stance:** Fuse uncommons and commons regularly with gold. Do NOT use gems for fusion — save gems for Wheel and Card Master.
