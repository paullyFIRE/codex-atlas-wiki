## Necromancer

### Overview
Pair with Goddess of War to keep skeletons alive. Excels in PvE. Skeletons melt dragon HP.

### Stats (Level 4)
- ~32K HP (decent survivability)
- Medium tank stats for a backline troop
- Skill unlocks at level 4

### Performance
- Defense with two level 4 Necromancers pushed Clown to 6,360 trophies (highest ever)
- On clan war dragon: ~176 morale loss per hit vs ~121 normal, but 3.6M damage possible
- Skeletons require Goddess of War shielding to survive dragon AoE
- Excellent in Infinite War and hunting

### Placement
- Both Necromancers on the strong side split
- Place in back — you want them surviving
- Back front formation with Pretty Boy protecting from backstabs

### Verdict
| Use Case | Rating |
|----------|--------|
| PvE (Hunting, IW, Dragon) | S-tier |
| PvP | A-tier (with Goddess support) |
| Level to aim for | 4 (light/medium spender), 6+ (heavy spender) |

## Light Seeker (Pretty Boy)

### Overview
Stronger raw damage than Necromancer in PvP. Multiple copies stacked together shred enemies.

### Abilities
- **Holy Wash** (innate): Continuous AoE damage to nearby enemies + heals self when HP below 50%. Healing restores ~50% of HP (e.g., 16,101 heal on ~32K HP).
- **Light Volley** (unlocks level 4): Multi-shot + 50% attack buff when skill triggers.
  - "50% more clip, 50% more damage clip" — ~1,000 damage boost at level 4

### Performance
- "People with 3, 4, 5 level 4/5/6 Light Seekers absolutely shred when they trigger"
- Considered a "problem" by round table — power spike disproportionately strong
- Sloppy formations with Light Seekers can beat well-constructed ones

### Placement
- Keep in back for protection
- Can be used for backstab defense
- Let Goddess of War tank while Light Seeker dishes damage

### Verdict
| Use Case | Rating |
|----------|--------|
| PvP | S+ (multiple copies) |
| PvE | A-tier (good single target) |
| Level to aim for | 4 minimum, higher is devastating |

## Ripple Wizard

### Overview
Energy battery for your strongest troops. Hard to get — only from scrolls.

### Ability: Aqua Revival
- Grants moderate energy to nearby allies in a LARGE area
- Triggers every 2.5 seconds
- Level 6: Grants small amount of BONUS energy when skills trigger

### Best Pairings
| Troop | Why |
|-------|-----|
| **Storm Maiden** | Energy = more storms |
| **Frost Queen** | Energy = more freezes |
| **Starlight Apostle** | Energy = more healing/buffing |
| **Jungle Ranger** | Energy = more attacks |
| **Bone Marksman** | Energy = more bone shots |
| **Light Seeker** | Energy = more holy wash triggers |
| **Goddess of War** | Energy = more shields |
| **Bone Gunner** | Energy = more damage |

Also helps (honorary members since usually out of range):
- Backline troops still get their ability boosted

### Acquisition
- Scrolls only
- Not from chests
- Not in wheel
- Often paired with weaker troops in summon pools (Fury, Geomancer)

### Verdict
High priority scroll target. Even at level 4, provides significant value. Level 6 is the goal.

## Bone Marksman

### Overview
"Pseudo level-6 at level 4." Stat scaling doubles 1→2→3→4, then drops to 25-30% per level. Considered the best unit in the game overall.

### Stats
| Level | HP | Attack | Notes |
|-------|----|--------|-------|
| 3 | 16K | 1K | Comparable to level 4 Mist Archer |
| 4 | 32K | 2K | Skill unlocks — equivalent to level 6 Ronin in HP, stronger than level 6 Elven Archer in damage |

### Ability: Bone Shot
- Every 8 seconds, fires a piercing bullet
- Prioritizes enemy marksmen and mages
- 250% damage coefficient (~5K damage per hit at level 4)
- Long range (slightly shorter than Mist Archer)

### Performance Evidence
| Matchup | Result |
|---------|--------|
| Level 4 vs Silver Horde L25 | 120K damage, highest on team |
| Level 4 vs Omaji's L20 | 250K damage |
| Level 3 vs level 3 Elven Archer | Barely loses (1-2 hits short) |
| Level 4 vs level 6 Ronin | Equal HP, better damage |

### Comparison: Bone Marksman vs Flame Duelist
Both are heavy hitters. Comparable at similar levels. Bone Marksman has better range/utility.

### Placement Guide
- **Front row** recommended — wants to start shooting immediately
- Front row in split: place on right side (money spot) — when left side wins, right-side Bone Marksman shoots into enemy backline first
- Don't place past row 4 — too much delay before first shot
- Syncs with attack speed reduction troops (Iron Bulwark, Frost Skeleton) — Bone Shot fires twice if target has low attack speed
- **Never merge your last two copies** — two level 4s >>> one level 5

### Verdict
| Use Case | Rating |
|----------|--------|
| PvP | S-tier (targets backline) |
| Hunting | S-tier (consistent top damage) |
| Clan War Towers | A-tier |
| Level to aim for | 3 minimum, 4 ideal, 6 for power |

## Multi-Unit vs Single-Unit Mechanics

A critical mechanic that affects how you evaluate troops:

**Multi-units** (e.g., Berserker, Demoman, Swordsman): The displayed HP/ATK is per INDIVIDUAL troop in the squad, not the total. A level 6 Berserker has 8 individuals, each with the displayed HP and ATK. Combined, the squad has ~36K HP and ~800 ATK.

**Single units** (e.g., Goblin Chef, Wood Shield Guard): The displayed HP/ATK IS the actual stat for that one troop.

### Implications
- **Multi-units** are better for damage dealing and triggering abilities multiple times since each individual attacks separately
- **Single units** perform better as heavy hitters or tanks — higher concentrated stats
- Example: Goblin Chef has 23K HP / 1,200 ATK as a single unit; each individual Berserker has ~4,600 HP (dies quickly), but combined squad has more total HP

## Rare Troop Guide

Most rares fall off once you get level 6+ epics/legendaries. A few are worth investing in long-term.

### Range Units

| Troop | Verdict | Reason |
|-------|---------|--------|
| **Forest Scout** | **Best rare range** | Highest attack of the three. Multi-target ability deals 200% damage. At level 7 with Trigger Master, "melts butter." Place away from other archers in safe back position. |
| **Apprentice Mage** | Pass | Fire damage barely matters — level 7 Mage (300 ATK) vs level 6 Scout (280 ATK) against fire-weak Pumpkin Guard only does ~5% more. Replaced by Flame Mage. Don't waste resources. |
| **Goblin Shaman** | Skip | Must unlock in wheel first. By time you can fuse and upgrade, you'll have Woodland Wizard who replaces him. Not worth the investment loop. |

### Melee Units

| Troop | Verdict | Reason |
|-------|---------|--------|
| **Berserker** | Skip | Low value |
| **Werewolf** | Skip | Low value |
| **Goblin Warrior** | Useable | High HP, decent damage from ability. Not top priority. |
| **Flayer Warden** | **Best rare melee** | Lower stats than Goblin Warrior but splash damage handles multi-units well. New players should play more Wardens. |

### Tanks

| Troop | Verdict | Reason |
|-------|---------|--------|
| **Paladin** | Good | Multi-unit with high HP and surprisingly high ATK. Generates shield every 10s. Combos with Radiant Warrior: Radiant's shield lasts 8s (starts at battle), Paladin's shield activates at 10s — creates a 2s overlap. |
| **Goblin Shredder (Chef)** | **Better than level 6 mythic** | Single unit — high HP and very high ATK for a tank. Splash damage + **2s stun** (longer than Griffin Rider at 1s and Ursa at 0.5s). At level 9: HP near level 6 Night King, ATK near Light Seeker, for half the deployment points. ~1,000 forge stones to get to level 9. Only downside: very hard to get copies (needs wheel/card master unlock + fusion). |

### Utility Rares

| Troop | Verdict | Reason |
|-------|---------|--------|
| **Bomber** | **Excellent** | Suicide unit. Ability deals 3x base ATK. At level 7: stuns on death. Best in backstab/outflank strategies. In split formations vs backstabs: place front close to corner, otherwise he runs toward stabbers instead of enemy front line. |
| **Frost Skeleton** | **Excellent utility** | On death, deals area damage + applies speed reduction. Can be placed up front (slow immediately), middle (delayed slow), or back (long delayed slow). Best paired with Bone Marksman, Bone Gunner, Bone Warden, Iron Bulwark (attack speed synergy). |

### Verdict
Only invest forge stones in: **Forest Scout**, **Flayer Warden**, **Goblin Shredder**, **Paladin**, **Bomber**, **Frost Skeleton**. Skip the rest.

## Woodland Guardian (Tank/Healer)

### Acquisition
- Only from Wheel or Card Master

### Stats & Ability
- Low HP and ATK for a tank (offset by self-healing)
- Heals every 12 seconds: 30% HP + fixed amount (3,000 HP at level 4)
- Healing radius: ~1.2 square radius
- Affects nearby allies AND self

### Placement by Formation Type

| Formation | Placement | Notes |
|-----------|-----------|-------|
| Backstab/flanks vs backstab/outflanks | Back, off to side | Follows upside-down J path through range units, healing them |
| Split vs split | Strong side, outer corner tile | Ensure that side has good tanks + heavy hitters |
| Split vs backstab/outflanks | Farthest back | Acts as second wave of tanks. Strong front line required. |
| **Never** place on weak side | — | Won't survive |

### Deployment Point Requirements

| Command Center | Deployment Points | Guardian Viability |
|----------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| CC2 | 150 | Only if level 4 (8 cost is too high for low level) |
| CC3 | 200 | One level 3, preferably level 4. Can do two level 4s if composition allows |
| CC4+ | 250 | Two level 3s or level 4s, whatever fits |

### Mode-Specific

**Evil Ivy Hunt:**
- Minimum level 2
- Pair with Radiant Warrior, Goddess of War, Woodland Wizard
- Goddess of War can also target him if he's near warrior-type troops
- Stun phase: place Woodland Wizards right next to Guardian
- Knockback phase: spread Woodland Wizards out — when he gets knocked back and returns, one Wizard will be in healing range

**Infinite War:**
- Minimum level 3 with Radiant Warrior
- Level 4 can be by themselves
- Focus him where your strongest tanks are — he keeps them alive longer

## Common Troop Guide

### Level 7 Jump
Level 7 commons get a massive power spike: higher HP/ATK AND unlock the Trigger Master passive ability. This is a huge jump from level 6.

### Forge Stone Policy for Commons
- **Only upgrade one of each troop to level 7** — except Wood Shield Guard
- Every troop to level 7 gives a Power Stance achievement = **100 gems**
- Don't waste forge stones on commons you won't use long-term
- Save forge stones for Ursa, Elven Archer, Oracles, Flame Mages

### Common Troop Evaluations

**Melee:**
| Troop | Verdict | Reason |
|-------|---------|--------|
| **Swordsman** | Better choice | Splash damage in skill radius. Multi-unit. |
| **Demoman** | Inferior | Stun only hits one target. Mirror match always loses to Swordsman. |
| **Wood Shield Guard** | **Trash** | Single unit (not multi). Alone is useless. Skip entirely. |

**Range:**
| Troop | Verdict | Reason |
|-------|---------|--------|
| **Archer** | **Best** | Highest ATK, multi-target skill. Most reliable damage. |
| **Gunner** | Pass | Fire damage too low to matter even against fire-weak enemies. |
| **Snowball Thrower** | Pass | Water damage irrelevant. Slow effect on skill is useless. |

### When to Use Commons
- As a new player with low deployment limit, you'll use commons
- Replace with rares as soon as possible
- In rare cases where you have 2 deployment points left: Swordsman is the best common melee, Archer is the best common range

## Lucky Dice Co-op Strategy (Event)

From external event guide — strategy for reaching wave 64 as level 5 with one mythic, or wave 80+ when fully stacked.

### Gold Mine Production Targets

| Player Level | Mine Production Per Level |
|-------------|--------------------------|
| Beginner | 5 / 9 / 10 / 10 |
| Higher Level | 5 / 8 / 9 / 9 |

### Early Game
1. Recruit 7 troops (stack of 3 for upgrade, possible task completion for 5 common types = 80 silver)
2. Invest in mine until it produces 5 gold coins
3. Continuously play dice game for chance at 4 silver coins
4. Pay attention to mine level — invest per the target table above

### Late Game (Wave 40+)
- With mine-first strategy: have 5+ legendary troops by wave 40, first mythic soon after
- Legendary/mythic troops reach max level by wave ~70
- Transcend legendary units when you have two of them
- Mine-first strategy gives higher level legendary/mythic units compared to recruit-first

### Key Tip
As you pass the finish line in the dice game, it upgrades — allowing chances at better tier troops.

## Oracles

### Overview
Pound-for-pound best epic in the game. Attack buffer.

### How They Work
- Each Oracle provides 4% attack buff to nearby allies
- Buff stacks up to 20 layers
- Icon appears on buffed troops (yellow attack symbol)
- Large skill range at level 7 is critical

### Stacking Math

|---------|------|------------------------|
| 1 | 4% | 1,464 |

### Placement
- Group them together with tanks/DPS in range
- Level 7 (large skill range) dramatically increases coverage
- Protect with Goddess of War shields
- Keep them alive — dead Oracles give no buffs

### Verdict

## Card Master System

### Overview
Alternative to the wheel for acquiring mythic troops. Costs 250 gems per pull.

### How It Works
- 12 cards face down
- 1 contains the featured mythic
- Draw without replacement — first pick 1/12, second 1/11, etc.
- Average 6.5 picks to find the mythic
- Chest rewards at 10, 30, 60 pulls give additional mythics + resources
- The chest rewards significantly reduce effective cost

### Cost Analysis
| Goal | Raw Pulls | With Chest Bonuses |
|------|-----------|-------------------|
| 1 Mythic | 6.5 avg | Less with reward chests |
| 32 Mythics (→ level 6) | ~208 pulls | Significantly less |

### Strategy
- Good for targeting specific mythics (Mist Archer, Frost Queen)
- Use when the featured troops are S-tier
- Costs are comparable to wheel when factoring chest rewards
- Stop at chest milestones for efficiency

## Recruitment & Mastery System

### Level 1 vs Level 3 Recruiting
There is a significant difference in mastery points earned:

| Recruit Level | Cost | Mastery Points | Epics Received |
|--------------|------|---------------|----------------|
| Level 1 | 1x | 1 per card (~2 per combine) | Fewer epics |
| Level 3 | 4x (need 4 level 1s to make) | 0 (skip combine steps) | More epics |

### Key Insight
- Recruiting level 1s and combining them gives mastery points toward mastery chests
- Recruiting level 3s skips the combining steps, sacrificing mastery points
- Level 3 recruiting gives more epics per gem/gold
- Level 1 recruiting builds mastery chest progress

### Mastery Chests
- Progress bar fills as you combine troops (1 point per troop card)
- Chests contain rewards including troops and resources
- Epics give more mastery points than rares when combined

### Recommendation
Balance based on needs:
- Need mastery chests? Recruit level 1s and combine up
- Need epics fast? Recruit level 3s
- Best approach: mix of both

### Gold Cap Strategy
- Castle level 5: 3M gold cap
- Castle level 4: 2.5M gold cap
- Castle level 3: 2M gold cap
- Stay near cap → use gold on recruitment
- When near cap, recruit to bring gold down
- Keep gold just below max to not waste natural regen
