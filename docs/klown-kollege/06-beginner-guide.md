# Beginner Guide

---

## Daily Routine

### At Daily Reset (5PM Mountain / 7PM Eastern / Midnight GMT)

**Step 1: Collect Buildings**
- Go to building icon (bottom left)
- Collect: Gold Mine (gold), Sawmill (wood), extra wood building, Castle (gems)
- Start next upgrade (always castle first)

**Step 2: Complete Daily Quests**
- 5 daily quests per day (24 hours to complete, 3 resets if you don't like one)
- Quests include: deal X damage with specific troops, win battles, etc.
- If a quest needs troops you don't have or can't do: RESET it
- You get 3 resets per day — use them wisely
- Also: weekly quests + seasonal quests (2-4 weeks duration)

**Step 3: Free Battles**
- 3 free Hunting battles per day
- 3 free Infinite War battles per day
- After 3, it costs gems — NOT worth it unless you're at the very top

**Step 4: Collect Free Stuff**
- Claim free items from shop
- Check events tab for limited-time rewards
- Sign in daily for event rewards (scrolls, chests, Frost Queen)

## What to Prioritize

### Early Game (First Month)

1. **Castle upgrades** — #1 priority. Spend all wood on castle. Each level = +50 troop energy.
2. **Oracles** — Get as many as possible. Best epic in the game. 4% attack buff per Oracle.
3. **Poison Masters** — 8-10 level 6 Poison Masters carry through mid-game Infinite War.
4. **Bombs** — Fusion uncommons with gold. Dominant in 2,500-5,000 trophy range.
5. **Goddess of War** — Essential support in every formation.

### Mid Game (1-3 Months)

1. **Radiant Warrior** — S+ tier. Get 2, keep them at same level.
2. **Frost Queen** — S+ tier. Same as above.
3. **Bone Marksman** — Level 4 minimum. Skill unlocks at 4.
4. **Light Seeker** — PvP destroyer.
5. **Necromancer** — PvE king.

### Late Game (3+ Months)

1. Level 7 Ursa and Elven Archer
2. Level 8+ epics (Oracles, Flame Mages)
3. Level 9 rares (Forest Scouts, Shove Boy, Bombs)
4. Ruler system in clan war
5. Multiple S+ tier mythics at level 4+

## Gem Spending (Updated Tier List)

### Do Spend On
1. **The Wheel** — Best value, especially stopping at 50/100 milestone chests
2. **Card Master** — Comparable to wheel when factoring chest rewards
3. **30-Day Gem Pass ($15/mo)** — Best real-money value

### Don't Spend On
1. Extra Infinite War/Hunting battles
2. Fusion (gems only, gold is fine)
3. Recruit / Summon with gems
4. Any non-mythic wheel

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### Mistake #1: Using Forge Stones on Wrong Troops
**Correct:** Save forge stones for Ursa, Elven Archer, Oracles, Flame Mages.
**Wrong:** Wasting on commons/rares you won't use long-term.

### Mistake #2: Not Picking a Dragon Clash Side
The event runs periodically. Pick a side IMMEDIATELY. Missing even one day of quests wastes rewards.

### Mistake #3: Merging Troops Too Early
**NEVER merge your last two copies of a strong troop.**
- Keep two Radiant Warriors (don't merge into one)
- Keep two Frost Queens (don't merge into one)
- Two level 3s > one level 4
- Exception: when you have a third copy

### Mistake #4: Spending Gems on Wrong Things
- Extra battles are worthless
- Fusion with gems is outdated advice
- Save gems for Wheel + Card Master only

### Mistake #5: Forgetting Name Color Trick
Your name can have colors! Use `<#RGB>` format with 3-digit hex codes from borderleft.com/toolbox/hex

### Mistake #6: Ignoring Trial Feature in Clan War
You can hit towers/gates/dragon for FREE to test formations. No morale cost.

### Mistake #7: Not Coordinating Dragon Times
Dragon diplomacy is key in clan war. Work with neighbors to share dragon windows.

### Mistake #8: Not Using Rope-a-Dope
In Crimson Moon, leave tower → let enemy hit → return as attacker for advantage.

### Mistake #9: Spending All Gold at Once
Stay near gold cap but don't waste natural regen. Use gold on recruitment when near cap.

### Mistake #10: Using Uncommons in Your Main Formation
"These blow ass. No one uses these." Fuse them away for a chance at Bombs.

### Mistake #11: Wasting Troop Recruitment on Level 3s Only
Level 1 recruiting gives mastery points toward mastery chests. Level 3 recruiting skips this. Balance both.

### Mistake #12: Not Checking Discord for Event/Troop Schedules
Official Discord has `+wheel`, `+summon`, `+cardmaster` commands showing upcoming schedules.

### Mistake #13: Not Understanding Multi-Unit vs Single-Unit Stats
Health and attack shown is per individual troop in a squad, NOT the total. A Berserker with 8 individuals shows per-troop stats. A single-unit like Goblin Chef shows actual stats. This changes how you evaluate troops.

### Mistake #14: Wasting Forge Stones on Level 7 Commons
Level 7 commons have Trigger Master and are a big jump, but forge stones are too precious. Only upgrade one of each common to level 7 for the 100-gem Power Stance achievement. Save stones for Ursa, Elven Archer, Oracles.

### Mistake #15: Using Wood Shield Guard
Wood Shield Guard is a single-unit common — trash tier. Never use or upgrade. Use Swordsman instead (multi-unit with splash damage).

### Mistake #16: Using Snowball Thrower or Gunner
Archer is the only common range worth using. Fire/water damage on commons is too low to matter. Snowball Thrower's slow effect is useless.

## Progression Path

### Week 1-2
- Focus castle upgrades
- Complete all dailies
- Save gems (don't spend)
- Get Oracles and Poison Masters

### Week 3-4
- Start using gems on wheel (good mythic rotations)
- Build up Bombs via fusion
- Join active clan (Heretic Sanctuary or similar)
- Start co-op grinding

### Month 2
- Target specific mythics (Radiant Warrior, Frost Queen)
- Reach 2,500-5,000 trophies (Bombs territory)
- Participate in clan war (learn basics)
- Join Dragon Clash events

### Month 3+
- Level 7 epics/legendaries with forge stones
- Ruler system in clan war
- Multiple S-tier mythics at level 4+
- Top 1,000 arena
- Active in clan leadership/diplomacy
