# Clan War Guide

---

## Clan War 101

### Schedule
- Starts at reset on Saturday (USA) / Sunday (rest of world)
- 8 teams on a map, 8 castles
- Two main maps that rotate (plus a third sometimes)

### Basics
- Everyone starts at their castle on their color
- Map is colorless at start → your team is your color
- Free-for-all dash for towers at reset
- Towers start gray → capture them by attacking
- Paths are clearly outlined between towers

### Scoring
- **Metals** (clan points) — shown in tracker, from tower captures and holding
- **Dragon hits** — heavily skewed in tracking, best source of points
- Tracked in Clan Tracker feature (leaders can view activity per season)

### Key Objectives
| Objective | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Towers** | Capture and hold for points. Level 1-5 towers. |
| **Gates** | Block access to areas. Must be taken to reach dragon. |
| **Dragon** | Opens every ~12 hours for 2 hours. Massive points. |
| **Spider** | Bonus PvE objective on some maps. |
| **Castle** | Your home base. Protect it. |

### Morale
- Morale is spent when you attack
- Attacking with PvP formation on a tower loses more morale than attacking with a tower buster
- Defenders lose ~30% morale in Crimson Moon (attackers have the advantage)
- Dragon hits also cost morale (~120 per hit without Necromancer, ~176 with Necromancer)

## Dragon Strategy

### Dragon Schedule
- Opens every ~12 hours for 2 hours
- Only the team holding the gate can hit the dragon
- Coordinating with neighbors/allies is common

### Dragon Diplomacy
Teams coordinate dragon attack times to share points:
- "VNF had the first 30 minutes"
- "Easy took over the gate"
- "Now it's our turn"

**How to coordinate:**
1. Work with 3-4 neighboring teams on the map
2. Agree on time slots (e.g., 30 min per team)
3. Take the gate when it's your turn
4. Pummel the dragon for your window

This avoids constant fighting and ensures everyone gets points. Not everyone will cooperate — strong teams may take what they want.

### Dragon Damage
- Necromancer can deal massive damage: screenshots show 3.6M damage on dragon
- But Necromancer costs ~176 morale vs ~121 normal hit
- Goddess of War shields keep Necromancer skeletons alive longer
- Trial hits are free — test formations before committing morale

## Ruler System

### Overview
The Ruler system adds a commander unit to your formations in clan war.

### How to Equip
1. Go to Barracks
2. Click Ruler button (bottom right of formation)
3. Click "Use" to equip the ruler
4. Save formation
5. Must be done for EACH formation separately

### Core Stones
Core stones are how you get weapon fragments for your ruler. Think of it like co-op troop recruitment:

| Core Stone Level | Higher Rarity Fragments |
|-----------------|------------------------|
| Level 1-5 | Mostly 1-star pieces |
| Level 6-7 | Sweet spot — good odds of 2-3 star pieces |
| Level 8+ | Higher chance of rare fragments |

**Upgrade risk:** 50% success rate to upgrade core stone. Failure may decrease level.

### Weapons
- Collect 9 pieces per weapon (shown as X of 9)
- Once all pieces collected, click "Activate" to equip
- Different star levels (1-star, 2-star, 3-star)
- Multiple ruler slots (more rulers possible in future)

### Strategy
- Get core stone to level 6-7 before spending fragments
- Level 6-7 is the sweet spot for efficiency
- Upgrade attempts have 50% success rate
- Higher core stone level → better fragments

## Rope-a-Dope Technique

A PvP trick for Crimson Moon and general tower defense:

**The technique:**
1. Enemy approaches your tower
2. Move your formation OUT of the tower
3. Enemy hits the tower with their PvP formation
4. Move your formation BACK into the tower
5. Now YOU are the attacker (you have advantage)
6. Switch to your tower-busting build
7. Attack them while they're on your tower

**Why it works:**
- Attackers have advantage in Crimson Moon (defenders lose ~30% morale)
- Enemy loses extra morale from hitting a tower with PvP formation
- You get a "hit and a half" on them

## Trials & Formation Advice

### Free Trial Feature
- Click any tower, gate, dragon, or spider on the map
- Click the hamburger menu (three dots)
- Select "Trial"
- Hits are FREE — no morale cost, no movement cost

### What to Test
- How fast can you take down level 1 vs level 5 towers?
- What formation hits dragon hardest?
- What composition works best for spider?
- Can you tweak formations to improve performance?

### Key Insight
Trial gives you risk-free data. Always trial before committing morale on real attacks. Top players use this extensively to optimize.

## Clan War Diplomacy

### Diplomacy Tab
- Clan leader can label other teams as Enemy, Neutral, or Ally
- Labeling an enemy turns their towers RED on the map
- Enemies get a notification (silly AI-generated message)
- No agreement needed — it's one-sided
- Leaders can grant permissions to other officers

### Communication
Many successful teams coordinate with neighbors:
- "We've been working very well with three of our neighbors"
- "Sharing dragon" — fight before, fight after, but share dragon for points
- 4v4 maps emerge when teams form alliances
- Shout outs to: Regulators (18+), Rock (Korean team), Brotherhood, MJ, SF
